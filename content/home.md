# Convivy

Convivy is a software lab dedicated to agentic engineering. We are building an agentic development platform to make us better at building commercial software products. Building those commercial software products makes us better at building our agentic development platform. Each of our two outputs helps us get better at the other.

## [Convivy Builder](https://convivybuilder.org)

The agentic development platform we build our products with. It writes all of our code.

## [Vivy](https://vivy.rentals)

For short-term rental hosts: know your turnovers are happening as planned.
