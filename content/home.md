# Convivy

Convivy is a software engineering lab. We make commercial products for small businesses and individuals, in domains we know well. We also make tools for agentic development, including tools that we ourselves use to develop products.

Building each one of those things makes us better at building the other.

## [Convivy Builder](https://convivybuilder.org)

The agentic development platform we build our products with.
