## Write Better

[github.com/convivy/write-better](https://github.com/convivy/write-better)

The writing standard everything here is held to, ours and the models'. It names the habits that make AI-drafted prose recognizable — defining a thing by what it isn't, making the same point three times at narrowing zoom, the fragment standing in for a sentence — and gives the plainer form to use instead. Free, MIT.
